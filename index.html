"Harmony in Diversity (中外和美之家)"

Our dormitory theme, "Harmony in Diversity," creates a living symbol of cross-cultural friendship by elegantly blending traditional Chinese elements with decorations from our home countries. Through this thoughtful design, we aim to showcase a beautiful, unified living space that truly embodies the spirit of global community and learning.
